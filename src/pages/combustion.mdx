## From injector to nozzle

We turn the rocket’s size and performance goals into the geometry of the engine.
Calculations and simulations guide the sizing of the injector, combustion
chamber, throat, and nozzle. Each decision has to account for the propellants,
operating pressures, and intense heat the hardware will face.

## From design to hardware

The work moves between CAD, modeling, and the machine shop. We manufacture
engine components and bring the designs together as physical hardware ready for
testing. Current development focuses on an ablative chamber to support rapid
testing, with a lining that gradually wears away to protect the chamber from
combustion heat.

## Bringing the test together

Combustion Devices works with Fluid Systems and Electrical & Controls to match
the engine with its propellant supply and test setup. Beyond the engine itself,
we help develop the ignition system, test stand, and water deluge system that
limits exhaust damage. Preparing for a hot fire means making those pieces work
together.
