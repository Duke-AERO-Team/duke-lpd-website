## From tank to injector

We bring fuel, oxidiser, and pressurant to the engine through a connected system
of tanks, regulators, and plumbing. The layout has to account for both the flow
paths and how the hardware fits around the test stand. Our work connects the
propellant supply to the conditions the engine needs.

## Designing the tanks

The propellant tanks are our own design rather than bought hardware. Each is a
cylinder closed by a machined dome that carries the ports on its face, seals
against the tank wall on a pair of O-ring grooves, and takes the bolt load
through a ring of lugs around its rim. The closures start in CAD and finish on
the lathe, turned from solid stock in the same shop as the engine parts.

## Routing & control

The piping and instrumentation diagram maps how the system connects, from
pressurisation to the engine inlet. Servo ball valves, three-way valves, and
check valves manage the flow paths, while pressure transducers and thermocouples
provide measurements. We work with Electrical & Controls to connect that
hardware to the test sequence and shutdown controls.

## Building the connections

The work comes together on the stand, where tanks, lines, valves, and sensors
become one system. Fluid Systems works alongside Combustion Devices to connect
the feed system to the engine, and alongside Electrical & Controls to make it
operable. Shared shop work and integration bring the plumbing, engine, and
electronics together for testing.
