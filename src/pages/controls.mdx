## Electronics built for the stand

We design, assemble, and program the circuit boards that connect the test
stand’s sensors and valves. The work spans circuit design, board layout, wiring,
and firmware—bringing individual components together into a system that can
command hardware and read back what it is doing.

## Test control & data

Our software gives operators a way to command valves, run test sequences, and
stop a test when needed. Alongside those controls, we collect and display sensor
readings so the team can follow conditions as a test runs and review the data
afterward. Hardware, firmware, and the operator interface all have to work
together.

## Working across the subteams

Electrical & Controls works with Fluid Systems and Combustion Devices to connect
the electronics to the physical stand. Bench checks, troubleshooting, and
integration are as much a part of the work as designing a board or writing code:
the goal is a setup the whole team can operate and learn from.
